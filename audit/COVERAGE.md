## Clarifications

### frameworks-theoretical
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 43, what function is assigned to the theoretical framework on PDF page 43?

A theoretical framework normally grounds the study in relevant theory and its assumptions. A legal basis is a separate justification, not a defining requirement of a theoretical framework.

- [USC Libraries: theoretical framework](https://libguides.usc.edu/writingguide/theoreticalframework)

### literature-primary-examples-slide
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 58, which examples are grouped as primary sources on Methods of Research, PDF page 58?

Source status depends on the evidence and research question, not publication format. Encyclopedias commonly synthesize earlier evidence. The same deck gives more useful firsthand/secondhand examples on pages 84-85.

- [Library of Congress: primary and secondary sources](https://ask.loc.gov/faq/303148)

### literature-secondary-examples-slide
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 59, which examples are grouped as secondary sources on Methods of Research, PDF page 59?

Being online or unpublished does not determine whether a source is primary or secondary. An original thesis can report primary research; online collections can preserve firsthand records. Classify by the role of the evidence.

- [Library of Congress: primary and secondary sources](https://ask.loc.gov/faq/303148)

### designs-quasi-example
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 78, the table places gender differences in visual/spatial abilities under which row?

The table labels this gender-difference example quasi-experimental. Comparing pre-existing groups without an intervention is often causal-comparative/non-experimental; group differences alone do not make a true experiment.

- [KPU: non-equivalent groups designs](https://kpu.pressbooks.pub/psychmethods4e/chapter/non-equivalent-control-group-designs/)

### descriptive-areas
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 90, which two broad areas of quantitative description are named on page 90?

Description or observed association alone does not establish causation. Causal conclusions require a design and assumptions that address alternative explanations.

- [BCcampus: experiment basics and validity](https://opentextbc.ca/researchmethods/chapter/experiment-basics/)

### descriptive-genetic-heading
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 94, which heading accompanies the paragraph called a genetic method on Methods of Research, PDF page 94?

The yellow paragraph appears mismatched with its heading: longitudinal development is different from the green box's comparison of similarities and differences. Use the quoted heading only for this source-specific item; do not infer that every comparative study is developmental.

- [University of Kansas: designs with comparison groups](https://opentext.ku.edu/assessmentevaluationhighered/chapter/chapter-14-designs-involving-comparison-groups/)

### experimental-dependent-wording

The independent variable is manipulated; the dependent variable is ordinarily measured as the response. The slide's dependent-variable wording should not be used as a general definition.

### approaches-external-validity-slide
According to The Architecture of Inquiry, PDF page 9, how does The Architecture of Inquiry, PDF page 9, describe EXTERNAL validity in its sidebar?

External validity concerns generalization beyond the studied people/settings. Extraneous variables and alternative causes concern internal validity. The sidebar conflates these concepts.

- [BCcampus: experiment basics and validity](https://opentextbc.ca/researchmethods/chapter/experiment-basics/)

### reliability-split-conflict
According to Methods of Research, PDF page 28, what administration wording appears for split-half in Methods of Research, PDF page 28?

Split-half reliability ordinarily uses one administration split into comparable halves. The supplied reliability deck page 7 uses that single-test approach. Two administrations are not required for split-half estimation.

- [UConn: instrument reliability](https://researchbasics.education.uconn.edu/instrument_reliability/)
- [ETS: reliability from comparable test halves](https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/report/1950/ikve.html)
